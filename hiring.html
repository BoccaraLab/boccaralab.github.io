1. Postdoctoral/Researcher position in Molecular and Systems Neurosciences through the NORPOD program

Boccara group is recruiting a highly motivated postdoctoral candidate for a 3-year NORPOD Postdoctoral Fellowship to study how sleep deficiencies may impact neural development and transcriptomics in rodent models. NORPOD is a collaborative postdoctoral program of the Nordic EMBL Partnership in Molecular Medicine. The NORPOD Fellow will be hired at the University of Oslo and join a collaboration at the Nordic EMBL Partnership institutes: Charlotte Boccara's group at NCMBM (University of Oslo, Norway) and Thomas Kim's group at DANDRITE (Aarhus University, Denmark).

The candidate must have a strong background in neurosciences and wet-lab experience in (i) systems neuroscience (with a focus on electrophysiology and sleep) and (ii) in spatial transcriptomics and high-resolution imaging.

Application deadline: 15 Aug, 2025.

2. Group leader at NCMBM:

Norwegian Centre for Molecular Biosciences and Medicine (NCMBM) seeks 1–2 early career Group Leaders on nine-year EMBL-style appointments. NCMBM is the Norwegian node of the Nordic EMBL Partnership for Molecular Medicine, alongside MIMS in Sweden, FIMM in Finland and DANDRITE in Denmark, bridging fundamental research and clinical innovation.
We welcome early- career scientists - typically ≤ 7 years post PhD - whose independent research program will complement and extend our two main research areas:

  1. Molecular and cellular basis of disease and therapy – human genetics and precision medicine, with emphasis on cancer, immunity and neurology
  2. Computational biology, functional genomics and systems biology – bridging genotype to phenotype
Application deadline: 8 Sep, 2025.
